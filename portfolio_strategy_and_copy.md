## Motion & Interaction Spec
*Strictly adhering to premium, high-performance CSS principles to guarantee 90+ mobile scores.*

### 1. Entrance Sequence (The Newsroom Boot)
*   **Step 1 [0ms]:** Sticky header blurs in using CSS backdrop-filter (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`), scaling opacity from `0` to `1`.
*   **Step 2 [200ms]:** Hero badge/pill slides up by 15px with high-damping spring ease (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`).
*   **Step 3 [400ms]:** Playfair display main heading fades in. Letter-by-letter opacity fade using pure CSS delay.
*   **Step 4 [600ms]:** Subheading and CTAs slide up into place.
*   **Step 5 [800ms]:** The background micro-grid subtly animates its opacity from `0` to `0.05` for a sleek modern touch.

### 2. Scroll-Linked Accents
*   **Reading Progress Bar:** A thin, high-contrast gold (`#F59E0B`) 2px bar at the very top of the window, scaling horizontally (`scaleX`) based on Lenis scroll position.
*   **Fading Gridlines:** As the user scrolls through sections, section separators (paper-thin borders) light up when they enter the center viewport and fade as they exit, preventing flat layout monotony.

### 3. Hover Micro-Interactions
*   **Magnetic Buttons:** The CTA buttons (`.magnetic-button`) use absolute coordinate tracking on mouse movements to pull towards the pointer by a capped distance of `10%` of their width, providing a highly premium tactile feedback.
*   **Bento Card Elevation:** On mouse hover, cards:
    *   Apply a scale transformation: `transform: scale(1.015) translateY(-4px)`.
    *   Transition a subtle amber glow drop-shadow: `box-shadow: 0 10px 30px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.08)`.
    *   Slide an icon arrow dynamically to the top-right.
*   **Menu Anchor Underline Reveal:** Floating navigation menu items feature a `::after` gold underline pseudo-element that scales from center outward on hover: `transform: scaleX(0) -> scaleX(1)` with a `0.3s cubic-bezier(0.25, 0.46, 0.45, 0.94)` ease.

### 4. Accessibility & Reduced Motion
*   When `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` is matched:
    *   Disable all custom scroll hooks (Lenis), reverting to standard instant browser scrolling.
    *   Eliminate `scale` and `translateY` animations from cards and buttons.
    *   Convert all magnetic behaviors into pure, standard `:hover` opacity alterations (`transition: opacity 0.15s ease-in-out`).
